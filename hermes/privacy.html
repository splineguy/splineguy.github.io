Privacy Policy — hermes-calendar

Last updated: October 24, 2026

hermes-calendar is a private, single-user automation tool operated by Scott Franklin. It is not a public service and has exactly one authorized user: the operator.

What it accesses

With the operator's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the tool accesses the operator's own Google account data:

  • Gmail messages and attachments
  • Google Calendar events
  • Google Drive files
  • Google Contacts
  • Google Sheets and Google Docs

Access is limited to the scopes each feature requires.

How the data is used

Accessed data is used solely to carry out tasks the operator requests — for example searching and summarizing mail, reading or creating calendar events, and reading or writing documents. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or analytics.

Storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the operator's own computer. Retrieved content is processed on that computer and, when a response is generated, passed to the AI model provider the operator has configured. No copy of the data is retained by any other party.

Third parties

There is no sharing beyond Google (the data source) and the AI model provider used to generate responses.

Your control

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
